Splits is a health and training app for people who run, ride, swim or race. It reads Apple Health and turns it into the three answers an athlete wants each morning: how ready am I, what should I do today, and how is the season going. Plans for running, triathlon, cycling, open water, HYROX and obstacle races reshape themselves around missed sessions, travel and weather. Every number says where it comes from. There is no account and no server of ours: your data stays in Apple Health and your own iCloud.

  • Adaptive plans for any endurance sport, set up from six questions
  • Readiness, recovery and the best hour to train, from sleep and heart rate variability
  • Nineteen measures Apple Health does not show, each with its published method
  • Every run, ride or walk replayed as a 3D flight over the terrain
  • An optional coach on Apple Intelligence that changes the plan from a sentence
  • Widgets, Live Activities and an Apple Watch app
