Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Splits collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours.

Apple Health

With your permission, Splits reads workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and other measures from Apple Health, and can write the workouts you tick off. This data is processed on your device and is never sent to us, sold, or used for advertising.

iCloud

If iCloud sync is on, your plan, race, settings and the sessions you log by hand are stored in your own private iCloud account so your devices match. We cannot see it.

Assistant

The coach uses Apple Intelligence on your device. If you choose Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini instead, you add your own key and agree first; a summary of the numbers the coach needs is then sent directly from your device to that provider under its own privacy policy. Nothing passes through us. Keys stay in your device's Keychain.

Children

Splits is not directed at children under 13.
