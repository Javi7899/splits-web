Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to beatsleepf@gmail.com. Replies within a few days.

Common questions

  • Readiness does not appear. Wear your Apple Watch to bed for a few nights; readiness compares each morning with your own normal.
  • A workout did not tick off. It ticks itself off when it reaches Apple Health, within a day of the planned session.
  • No health data on Mac. Apple Health is not available on Mac; your plan syncs through iCloud and the measures are on iPhone and iPad.

Splits is not a medical device and does not diagnose.
